You can show what's on your main screen on other screens around the building — a TV in the lobby, a tablet for the worship leader, or a second computer — over your church's Wi-Fi.

## Share over Wi-Fi from Studio mode

1. **Open the link.** In Studio mode, open **Settings** (or the arrow next to **Start output**) and choose **Share over Wi-Fi…**. Copy the link.
2. **Open it on the other screen.** Open the link in the web browser of a smart TV, laptop, phone or tablet, or add it to OBS as a Browser Source.
3. **Keep the Studio open.** The link shows what the Studio is sending, so it only works while Studio mode is open.

> [!TIP] No church Wi-Fi?
> Turn on a phone hotspot, connect both devices to it, and use the link the app shows.

## The presentation link (Free plan in OBS)

On the Free plan, the dock gives you a **presentation link** to add once to OBS as a Browser Source. See [Set up OBS Studio](/docs/obs-setup#how-scenes-and-sources-are-created).

## Phone remote control

A phone remote — moving slides and controlling the screen from your phone — is being prepared and isn't available in the app yet. When it arrives, this page will show how to pair your phone.
