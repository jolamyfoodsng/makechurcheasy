Broadcast graphics are animated lower thirds and overlays — a speaker's name and title, an announcement, a giving board, a social media bar. They animate in, stay for as long as you need, and animate out.

## Pick and customise a graphic

1. **Open the library.** Choose **Broadcast Graphics** in the sidebar. The **Template Library** tab has the ready-made designs.
2. **Customise it.** Choose **Customize** on a design. Change the text under **Content & Text** (you can fill it from your church profile), the colours under **Design & Colors**, and where it sits under **Position & Animation**.
3. **Save it.** Give it a **Graphic Name** and choose **Save & Add to OBS**. Your saved designs are in **My Graphics**.

## Show it in Studio mode

1. **Open the Graphics tab.** It's in the Studio side panel, or press **Cmd/Ctrl + G**.
2. **Choose a graphic.** Edit its text if needed.
3. **Set how it appears.** Choose a **Position**, a **Size**, and how long it **Stays for**: as designed, until you close it, or a set number of seconds.
4. **Choose Project.** Close it with **Close** or **Alt + G**.

## Show it in OBS

1. **Open the dock.** In OBS, go to **Ministry → Lower Thirds**.
2. **Pick the theme and fill in the text.**
3. **Choose Send to OBS.** Use **Animate Out** to take it off, or **Blank** to clear it straight away.

Animated graphics have a **Hold (seconds)** setting: how long they stay before animating out. Set it to keep a graphic up until you take it off.

> [!TIP] Don't see your new graphic in the dock?
> Right-click inside the dock in OBS and choose **Refresh**.
