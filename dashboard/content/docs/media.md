## Pictures, videos and documents

### Add media

- **In the app:** open **Resources → Media** and choose **Add Media**, or drag files onto the page. You can add pictures, videos, PDFs, Word (`.docx`) and PowerPoint (`.pptx`) files. Each page of a document becomes its own picture.
- **In Studio mode:** open **Media** and choose **Import Media** for pictures, videos and sound. For PDF, PowerPoint or Word files, open **Docs** and import there.

The Free plan holds up to 10 pictures and 5 videos. Paid plans have no limit.

### Show media

- **Studio mode:** select an item and choose **Send to Screen**. To run a slideshow, tick several items and choose **Start slideshow**. Documents show a page at a time, with **Fit to Screen** or **Fill**.
- **OBS dock:** open the **Media** tab, pick an item and choose **Send** (or **Send to Preview**).

> [!TIP] Videos that won't play
> Export the video as MP4 (H.264 video with AAC sound). It plays the most reliably.

## Countdowns and timers

Countdowns and timers are part of the **Growth** plan.

### In the app

Open **Countdowns & Timers**:

- **Designs:** create a countdown with **+ Create Countdown**, then **Play**, **Pause**, **Reset** and **Send to OBS**. Under **When Countdown Reaches Zero**, choose what happens next: nothing, switch OBS scene, hide the countdown, show a welcome graphic or play a video.
- **Rundowns:** plan the service as timers (count down, count up or time of day), with colours that change as time runs out, and a **Presenter screen** link for whoever is speaking.

### In the dock

Open **Ministry → Time**:

1. **Pick a timer.** Choose **Countdown**, **Rundown** or **Clock**.
2. **Start it.** Choose **Push & Start**.
3. **Adjust it live.** Use **Pause**/**Resume**, **Reset**, **+1 minute**, **-1 minute** and **+10 seconds**.
4. **Finish.** Choose **Stop & Remove** when you're done.

## Announcement tickers

A ticker scrolls short messages along the top or bottom of your OBS video.

1. **Open the ticker.** In the dock, go to **Ministry → Ticker**.
2. **Write your messages.** Add a **Heading** and your **Messages** (**Add Message** for each one).
3. **Set it up.** Choose the **Speed**, the **Position** (top or bottom), and whether it plays on a **Loop** or **Once**.
4. **Show it.** Choose **Send to OBS**. Use **Pause**/**Resume**, or **Clear** to take it off.

Tickers need OBS to be connected.

## Multi-view layouts

Multi-view puts several things on the screen at once — for example the preacher's camera beside the scripture. Multi-view is on paid plans and needs OBS.

1. **Start a layout.** In the dock, open **Multi-View** and choose **Add Multi-View**.
2. **Choose a Layout and a Background.**
3. **Add the content.** For each area, pick the OBS scene to show there.
4. **Frame it.** Choose **Fill**, **Fit** or **Custom** for each area.
5. **Review it.** Then choose **Apply to OBS**. MakeChurchEazy creates the scene in OBS for you.

You can also start from a ready-made design in **Multi View** in the app's sidebar, then **Add to OBS**.
