Verse AI listens to the preacher through a microphone. When it hears a Bible reference such as "Romans chapter eight, verse twenty-eight", it finds the verse for you. When it hears a well-known line such as "for God so loved the world", it suggests the verse it comes from.

> [!NOTE] What Verse AI needs
> Verse AI needs an internet connection, and it uses **credits** while it listens (about one credit a minute). Every plan includes some credits each month. You can see your balance on the **Credits** page.

## Start listening

1. **Open Verse AI.** Choose **Verse AI** in the sidebar, or press **Cmd/Ctrl + J** in Studio mode.
2. **Choose the microphone.** Pick the one the sermon comes through, and raise **Sensitivity** if it misses quiet speech.
3. **Choose Start Listening.** To stop, choose it again and confirm with **Yes, stop**.

## How verses reach the screen

Verse AI puts what it finds in two places:

- **Queue:** references the preacher clearly said, like "John 3:16".
- **Suggestions:** verses that match a line the preacher quoted.

You stay in control:

- **In Studio mode**, **Auto mode** only shows a verse by itself when Verse AI is very sure of a spoken reference. If **Preview first** is on, it waits in Preview until you send it. Suggestions are never shown by themselves. Double-click an item, or choose **Project**, to show it.
- **In the OBS dock**, **Auto-push queue to broadcast** is on while listening, so clear references go to the screen. Suggestions wait for you, unless you turn on **Auto-push suggestions to broadcast**.

> [!TIP] Check before it's seen
> Keep **Preview first** on in Studio mode, so each verse Verse AI finds waits for a quick look before the congregation sees it.

## Capture quotes for sermon notes

While Verse AI listens, the sermon appears as text in the **Transcript**. Right-click a line to **Add to sermon notes**, edit it or delete it. Saving to notes is on paid plans.

## Keep the transcript

When you stop listening, the session is saved. Find it under **Transcripts** in the sidebar, and use **Download Transcript** to save it as:

- **Plain text transcript** (`.txt`), or
- **Subtitles with timestamps** (`.srt`), for your video.
