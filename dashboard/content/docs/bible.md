You can show scripture from the **Bible** tab in the OBS dock or from **Bible** in Studio mode. Both use the same Bibles and work the same way.

## Find a verse

Type a reference into the search box. Short forms work, and so do small typos:

```text
John 3:16      jn3:16      j316
Rom 8:28-30    1cor13      ps23
gen1.1         ii cor 5
```

- **A range** like `Rom 8:28-30` shows several verses together.
- **Words** like `for God so loved the world` find the verse by its text.
- **Already in a chapter?** Type just a verse number (`18`, or `18-20`), or `+` and `-` to step forward and back.

Press **Enter** to pick the top result, or use **↑** and **↓** to choose another. Book names are recognised in English.

You can also browse: open the book and chapter picker, choose a book and chapter, and click a verse.

## Put it on the screen

### In the OBS dock

- **Click a verse** (or pick a search result) to send it to the screen straight away.
- **Move through the passage** with **↑** and **↓** (previous and next verse), **←** and **→** (previous and next chapter).
- **Full screen or lower third:** use **Full** and **LT** in the bottom toolbar.
- **Hide it for a moment** with **Hide Bible** (your place is kept). **Esc** clears the verse.

### In Studio mode

- **Click a verse** to see it in Preview, and **double-click** (or press **Enter**) to put it on the screen.
- **Several verses together:** hold **Shift** or **Cmd/Ctrl** and click to select them, or set **Verses per slide** (1 to 4).
- **Next verse:** press **Space** to show what's in Preview and move on to the next one.

See [Studio mode](/docs/studio-mode) for the screen controls and shortcuts.

## Bible versions

MakeChurchEazy includes the **KJV** and works offline with it. The first time you sign in with an internet connection, it also downloads **ASV**, **NKJV** and **ERV**.

### Download more versions

1. **Open the library.** Go to **Resources → Bible Library & Downloads**.
2. **Find a version.** In **Browse & Download**, search by name or language. The catalogue has around a thousand Bibles.
3. **Download it.** Downloaded versions appear under **Installed** and work offline.

You can also switch version from the search box by typing `@` and its short name, for example `@niv`. If it isn't downloaded yet, choose it to download it.

The Free plan holds a limited number of versions (delete one under **Installed** to make room). Paid plans have no limit. See [Pricing](/pricing).

## Compare translations or passages

### Two translations of the same verse

1. **Open the compare tools.** In the dock, open the menu and choose **Compare Translations & Passages**. In Studio mode, choose **Compare** next to the version picker.
2. **Choose the versions.** Turn on **Enable Compare Translations** and pick **Translation A** and **Translation B**.
3. **Choose a layout.** Pick **Line By Line** (one under the other) or **Side By Side**.

Shortcut: type `John 3:16 // niv` in the search box.

### Two different passages

In the **Passages** tab, turn on **Enable Compare Passages** and enter **Passage 1** and **Passage 2** (up to three), each in its own version. Under **Navigation**, choose whether moving forward moves all the passages together or only the active one.

## Change the look

- **In the dock**, choose **Quick Edits** to pick the theme. Full screen and lower third each keep their own theme. The **Quick** button changes the text size and the number of lines per verse.
- **In Studio mode**, choose a theme with **Cmd/Ctrl + T**, and change the text size in **Options**.

## Handy extras

- **Favorites and history:** with the search box empty, you'll see **Recent Searches** and **Favorites**. Right-click a verse and choose **Star Verse** to keep it.
- **Add to the schedule:** right-click a verse and choose **Add to Schedule**. See [plan the service](/docs/worship#plan-the-service-with-a-schedule).
- **Find verses as the preacher speaks:** see [Verse AI](/docs/verse-ai).
