Studio mode is MakeChurchEazy's own presentation screen. It sends scripture, worship lyrics, media and your camera straight to a projector or TV. You don't need OBS.

Open it from the dashboard with **Open Studio Mode**, or choose **Studio** in the sidebar.

## Put the output on your projector

1. **Connect the projector or TV.** Plug it into your computer, usually with HDMI.
2. **Choose Start output.** It's at the top of the Studio. With a second screen connected, the output opens full screen there. With only one screen, it opens in a window you can drag.
3. **Pick a different screen if you need to.** Open the arrow next to **Start output**, choose the **Screen**, then **Start output** (or **Move output** if it's already open). **Stop output** closes it.

> [!TIP] Closing the output
> Press **Esc** on the output screen to close it. Press **D** on it to see what it's receiving, which helps if it stays black.

The output is drawn at 1920 × 1080 and scales to fit your screen.

## Choose what goes on the screen

The Studio has five sections. Switch with the tabs or with **Cmd/Ctrl + 1–5**:

| Section | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Bible** | Verses and passages — see [Show Bible verses](/docs/bible) |
| **Text** | Worship songs and notes — see [Worship songs and hymns](/docs/worship) |
| **Media** | Pictures, videos, sound and slideshows — see [Media, countdowns and more](/docs/media) |
| **Docs** | PDF, PowerPoint and Word files, shown a page at a time |
| **Ministry** | A scrolling ticker, a countdown and a clock, on top of whatever is on the screen |

### Preview, then send

By default, clicking something puts it in **Preview** first, so you can check it. Press **Send** (or **Space**) to put it on the screen. The arrow next to **Send** switches between **Preview first** and **Straight to screen**.

**Clear** takes everything off the screen.

### The look on the screen

Choose how words and camera share the screen: **Full screen**, **Lower third**, **Camera**, or **Camera + lower third** (**Alt + 1–4**).

## Ticker, countdown and clock {#ministry}

The **Ministry** section has the same tools as the Dock's Ministry tab, for the Studio output:

- **Ticker** — type announcements, tick the ones to show, pick a theme, speed and top or bottom, then **Start ticker**. Changes go to the screen while it runs. **Pause** and **Stop** are next to it.
- **Countdown** — pick one of your countdown designs (made in **Countdowns & Timers**), set the length and **Start countdown**. You can pause, resume and add or take away time while it runs.
- **Clock** — choose a style, a corner or full screen, and **Show clock**.

They sit on top of the words, pictures and camera, and stay there until you stop them. A red dot on a tab means that tool is on the screen. The ticker and time tools are included with the Growth plan and above.

## Black screen and logo

- **Black screen:** press **Alt + B**, or right-click the monitor and choose **Black screen**. Choose **Show content again** to come back.
- **Logo:** press **Alt + L**, or use the **Logo** button. Choose **Edit** to pick your church logo, its size and the background behind it.

## The camera

The **Camera** card lists the cameras on this computer, and **NDI sources on the network** if NDI Tools is installed. Pick one and choose **Show**. Use **Refresh camera list** after plugging a camera in.

> [!NOTE] Camera access
> The first time, your computer asks to allow MakeChurchEazy to use the camera. If you said no, choose **Allow camera**, or turn it on in your computer's privacy settings.

## A second output

The **Alternate output** (on paid plans) opens a second screen showing the same item in a different look — for example a lower third for the livestream computer while the room sees full screen. Set it up from the arrow next to **Start output**: choose the **Words** look and the **Screen**.

## Share over Wi-Fi

Choose **Share over Wi-Fi…** to get a link that opens the output on a smart TV, phone, tablet or another computer on the same Wi-Fi, or in OBS as a Browser Source. See [Other screens and phones](/docs/mobile-remote).

## Run the service from a schedule

Open **Schedule** at the top. Pick or create a schedule, add items, and drag to reorder. Click an item to open it and double-click to put it on the screen. **Alt + N** moves to the next item.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **Cmd/Ctrl + /** in the Studio to see all shortcuts and change them. (On Windows, use **Ctrl** where you see **Cmd**.)

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `Space` or `Page Down` | Show Preview, then move to the next one |
| `Enter` | Send Preview to the screen |
| `Page Up` | Show the one before |
| `Cmd/Ctrl + Backspace` | Clear the screen |
| `Alt + B` / `Alt + L` | Black screen / Logo |
| `Alt + 1` to `Alt + 4` | Full screen, Lower third, Camera, Camera + lower third |
| `Alt + N` | Next item in the schedule |
| `Cmd/Ctrl + D` | Add to schedule |
| `Cmd/Ctrl + K` | Search everything |
| `Cmd/Ctrl + G` / `Alt + G` | Open graphics / Close the graphic on screen |
| `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + O` / `X` | Open / stop the output |

## Free plan

Everything you need for words, songs, the Bible and the output works on the Free plan. The Free plan has a daily allowance for a few extras, counted on this computer: turning the camera on (3 a day) and showing broadcast graphics (5 a day). Paid plans and the trial have no daily limits.
