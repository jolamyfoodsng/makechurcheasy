Your songs live in one library that the dashboard, the OBS dock and Studio mode all share. Add a song once and it's ready everywhere.

## Find your songs

- **In the app:** open **Resources → Worship** and the **Worship Songs** tab. Search by title, artist, words or hymn number (for example `Hymn 12`). Small typos are fine.
- **In the dock:** open the **Worship** tab and use **Search songs…**.
- **In Studio mode:** open **Worship**. The list has **All**, **Favorites**, **Recent** and **Custom** filters, and a star to mark favourites.

Songs you remove aren't lost: they go to the **Archive**, where **Restore song** brings them back.

## Add a song by typing or pasting

1. **Open the editor.** Choose **Add Song** (in the dock, **Add**).
2. **Give it a title.** Type a **Song Title** and, if you like, an **Artist**. If you leave the title as it is, the first line of the lyrics becomes the title when you save.
3. **Paste the lyrics.** Leave a blank line between verses: each blank line starts a new slide.
4. **Choose a layout.** Under **Slide Layout**, pick how many lines go on each slide (**1 Line** to **6 Lines**), or **Original** to keep your verses exactly as typed.
5. **Save.** Choose **Save Song**.

### Section labels

Put a label on its own line, or at the start of a verse, and the app names that slide. Any of these work:

```text
[Verse 1]
Verse 2:
<Chorus>
Bridge
2:
```

A number with a colon, like `2:`, means Verse 2. The app recognises **Verse** (or `V`), **Chorus** (or `C`, `Refrain`), **Pre-Chorus**, **Bridge**, **Tag**, **Intro** and **Outro**. A verse with no label becomes the next verse number.

### Tidy-up tools

The editor's toolbar can change letter case, **Clean** up spacing, **Trim Gaps** between lines, **Strip Numbers** (verse numbers), remove **Chords**, **Split here** to break a slide, and add a **+ Label**. **Wrap at** breaks long lines at 32, 46 or 64 letters.

## Import songs from a document

1. **Open the import.** In the app, choose **More tools → Import File**. In the dock, use **Import document**.
2. **Pick the file or paste text.** You can import PDF, Word (`.docx`), PowerPoint (`.pptx`), OpenDocument, HTML, Markdown or plain text. To paste instead, open **Paste Text** and choose **Detect Songs**.
3. **Review.** Each song found is listed with its title, sections and a preview. Fix anything, set songs to **Import** or **Skip**, then choose **Import Selected**.

> [!NOTE] Scanned PDFs
> A PDF that's a picture of the page (a scan) has no text to read. Export a searchable PDF, or paste the lyrics instead.

> [!NOTE] AI credits
> Hymn books and PowerPoint files are read on your computer. For other documents the app may use automatic structuring, which uses AI credits. The import tells you if you don't have enough.

### CCC hymnal

The Celestial Church of Christ hymnal PDF is recognised automatically. Each hymn is imported with its number, in Yoruba and English.

## Search for lyrics online

Choose **Search Online** (in the app it's under **More tools**), type at least three letters of a title, artist or line, or paste a Spotify track link. Pick a result, check the words, then choose **Import**.

Results come from public lyrics sites such as LRCLIB and several gospel lyrics sites. Always check the words before using them, and make sure your church has the right to project the song (for example through a CCLI licence).

## Present lyrics live

1. **Open the song.** In the dock's **Worship** tab, click a song to see its slides.
2. **Show a slide.** Click a slide to send it to the screen.
3. **Move through the song.** Use the arrow keys: **↓** or **→** for the next slide, **↑** or **←** for the previous one. **Enter** shows the current slide again.
4. **Clear the words.** Press **Esc**, or use **Hide lyrics**.

Switch between **Full** (full screen) and **LT** (lower third) in the dock's bottom toolbar. Right-click a slide to edit it, move it, or change the lines per slide just for this song.

In Studio mode, click a section to see it in Preview, and double-click to put it on the screen.

## Plan the service with a schedule

The schedule keeps everything for the service in order — songs, verses, media and notes.

1. **Open it.** In the dock, choose **Open Schedule**. Pick a schedule or choose **+ New Schedule…**.
2. **Add items.** Right-click a song, verse or media file and choose **Add to Schedule** (or **Add slide to Schedule** for a single slide).
3. **On Sunday, click each item in order.** A song opens at its first slide; use the arrow keys to move through it. The live item shows **Currently Live on Output**.

Pin the panel open so it stays visible during the service. The **History** tab shows what was on screen recently.

## Notes and announcements

Use **User's Notes** (next to **Worship** in the dock) for sermon points, announcements or anything else in text.

1. **Add a note.** Choose **Add Note**, give it a title and type the text. Leave a blank line between slides.
2. **Present it.** Click a slide to show it, use the arrow keys to move, and **Esc** to clear — just like songs.
