MakeChurchEazy puts Bible verses, worship lyrics, media and lower thirds on your church's screens. You can use it in two ways, and you can switch between them at any time:

- **Studio mode** sends everything straight to a projector or TV in the room. You don't need OBS.
- **With OBS Studio**, MakeChurchEazy works inside OBS for your livestream, through a dock panel and browser sources.

## Install the app

1. **Download MakeChurchEazy.** Go to [Download](/download) and choose the installer for your computer. There are installers for Windows, and for Macs with Apple silicon or Intel.
2. **Install and open it.** On a Mac, drag the app to Applications. On Windows, run the installer.
3. **Sign in.** The app shows three ways to sign in: **Continue in Browser**, a QR code you scan with your phone, or **Enter Pairing Code**. If you don't have an account yet, create one on the website when the browser opens.

> [!TIP] Pairing code
> To sign in with a code, get one from [your devices page](/devices) on the website, then type it into **Enter Pairing Code** (it looks like `ABCD-1234`).

## Choose how you'll present

The first time you open the app, it asks **How would you like to get started?**

| Choice | Best for | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MakeChurchEazy Studio** (Recommended) | Projecting in the room, with or without a livestream | Opens Studio mode straight away. No OBS needed. |
| **OBS Studio** | Churches that livestream with OBS | A short setup connects the app to OBS and adds the dock. |

You can change your mind later: the dashboard has an **Open Studio Mode** button, and OBS can be connected at any time from **Settings**.

## Your first service

1. **Open Studio mode.** Or open the dock if you use OBS.
2. **Find a verse.** In Bible, type a reference such as `John 3:16` or `Rom 8:28`. See [Show Bible verses](/docs/bible).
3. **Add your songs.** Paste lyrics or import a document. See [Worship songs and hymns](/docs/worship).
4. **Send it to the screen.** In Studio mode, choose **Start output** to open the projector screen, then send the verse or lyric live. See [Studio mode](/docs/studio-mode).

## Plans

MakeChurchEazy has a **Free** plan and paid **Basic** and **Growth** plans. The main differences:

- **Free** works in OBS through a *presentation link* that you add once as a Browser Source. The app doesn't create OBS scenes for you.
- **Basic and Growth** create and update the OBS scenes and sources automatically, and lift the limits on songs, media and Bible versions.
- **Growth** adds the most tools, including lower thirds and countdowns.

Limits change from time to time, so check [Pricing](/pricing) for the current details.

## What needs the internet

Most of a Sunday works offline once you're set up: the built-in KJV Bible, Bible versions you've downloaded, your songs and media, Studio mode and OBS control.

These need an internet connection: signing in, downloading Bible versions, Verse AI (speech to scripture), translation, online lyrics search and updates.

> [!NOTE] Staying signed in offline
> A paid plan keeps working offline, but the app needs to check in online at least every 21 days. Otherwise it falls back to the Free plan until it can check in again.
