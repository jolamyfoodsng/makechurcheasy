Welcome to MakeChurchEazy! We are thrilled to partner with your church and media team. You've just taken the first step toward running clean, distraction-free worship presentations and livestream broadcasts.

What is MakeChurchEazy? MakeChurchEazy is a modern church presentation platform built specifically for worship lyrics, Bible scriptures, media playback, and sermon notes.

Whether projecting directly to your sanctuary screens or sending lower-thirds straight into OBS Studio for your online livestream, everything syncs in real time with near-zero latency and ultra-low computer resource usage.

If you've used EasyWorship or ProPresenter, think of a much lighter, faster solution built natively around OBS Studio, AI-assisted scripture matching, and phone remote control.
Key Features You Now Have Access To
Live Speech-to-Scripture AI
No more frantically scrambling through books and chapters while the pastor speaks. The app listens to live audio and auto-detects referenced scriptures in real time.
Native OBS Browser Dock
Control lower-thirds, fullscreen scripture, countdowns, and themes straight inside your OBS Studio window without switching back and forth between applications.
Live Service Planner & Schedule Drawer
Pre-build your Sunday service order. Queue hymns, Bible verses, video announcements, and sermon points with 1-click stage projection.
Mobile Companion Remote
Control your presentation slides, switch Bible verses, and trigger cues directly from your phone or tablet on the church local network.
What should you expect next? 1. Download the Desktop App: Install MakeChurchEazy on your church computer (Windows or macOS).
2. Connect to OBS in 2 Minutes: Copy the Dock URL into OBS Custom Browser Docks and you are ready for rehearsal.
3. Smooth, Low-Resource Performance: Built to stay extremely lightweight so your computer fans stay quiet and your livestream encoders never drop frames.
